How to Sync External Audio with Video in mpv

This guide explains how to load an external audio track, replace or silence a video's embedded sound, and achieve millisecond-accurate synchronization in mpv. You will learn the essential command-line flags for loading separate tracks, how to mute the original track, and the precise keyboard shortcuts required to calibrate audio delay on the fly.

Loading External Audio via Terminal

To play a video file alongside a separate audio track, launch mpv from your terminal using the --audio-file option:

mpv "video.mp4" --audio-file="audio.mp3"

By default, mpv appends the external file as a secondary audio track. If your video already contains audio, mpv may continue playing the original stream. To ensure only the external audio plays, switch to the external track using the --aid (audio ID) option:

mpv "video.mp4" --audio-file="audio.mp3" --aid=2

If you do not know the exact track number, you can cycle audio tracks during playback by pressing # (Shift + 3) until the external track is selected.

Fine-Tuning Audio Synchronization in Real Time

If the audio does not match the video frames, you can adjust the timing in real time using built-in keyboard controls:

  • Ctrl + +: Adds a 100 ms delay to the audio (shifts audio later).
  • Ctrl + -: Subtracts 100 ms from the audio delay (shifts audio earlier).

Each press updates the On-Screen Display (OSD), displaying the current audio delay offset in milliseconds. Continue pressing the keys until mouth movements or distinctive sound cues align with the visual action.

Launching with a Fixed Audio Delay

Once you have identified the exact synchronization offset, you can apply it directly upon startup using the --audio-delay flag. The value is specified in seconds:

  • To delay audio by 250 milliseconds:

    mpv "video.mp4" --audio-file="audio.mp3" --aid=2 --audio-delay=0.25

  • To speed up audio by 150 milliseconds:

    mpv "video.mp4" --audio-file="audio.mp3" --aid=2 --audio-delay=-0.15

Drag-and-Drop Method

If you prefer using the graphical interface rather than the terminal:

  1. Open video.mp4 in mpv.
  2. Drag and drop the external audio file directly onto the running mpv window.
  3. Press # to cycle audio tracks until the newly loaded external audio is active.
  4. Use Ctrl + + and Ctrl + - to adjust the timing until synchronization is aligned.
