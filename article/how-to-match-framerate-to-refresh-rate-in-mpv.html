How to Match Framerate to Refresh Rate in mpv

This guide explains how to configure mpv to synchronize video framerates with your monitor's refresh rate to eliminate frame pacing judder. By adding display synchronization options to your configuration file, you can achieve smooth playback through audio resampling or frame interpolation. The following instructions walk you through locating your configuration file, setting the required options, and verifying that the synchronization is functioning properly.

1. Locate or Create your mpv.conf File

mpv reads user settings from a text file named mpv.conf. Open or create this file in the appropriate directory for your operating system:

  • Linux and macOS: ~/.config/mpv/mpv.conf
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\mpv\mpv.conf (or inside a portable_config folder located in the same directory as mpv.exe)

2. Configure video-sync Options

To match video timing directly to your display, open mpv.conf in a text editor and add the following lines:

# Match video timing to display refresh rate by resampling audio
video-sync=display-resample

# Optional: Enable interpolation to eliminate judder caused by mismatched rates
interpolation=yes
tscale=oversample

How These Options Work:

  • video-sync=display-resample: Adjusts the video timing to align precisely with your monitor's vertical refresh cycles. Audio speed is imperceptibly sped up or slowed down (drift) to prevent audio/video desynchronization without dropping frames.
  • interpolation=yes: Blends or blends-between frames when the video framerate does not evenly divide into your monitor's refresh rate (for example, playing 24 fps content on a 60 Hz display).
  • tscale=oversample: The algorithm used for interpolation. oversample is lightweight and artifact-free, though options like mitchell or bicubic can also be used depending on hardware performance.

3. Alternative Sync Modes

If you prefer not to resample audio, mpv supports alternate synchronization modes:

  • video-sync=display-vdrop: Drops or repeats entire video frames to maintain alignment with the display refresh rate without modifying the audio stream.
  • video-sync=display-adrop: Drops or duplicates audio samples to keep up with video synced to the display.

For the vast majority of systems, display-resample is the recommended option for jitter-free viewing.

4. Running via Command Line

If you want to test the setting without modifying your configuration file permanently, pass the arguments directly via terminal or command prompt:

mpv --video-sync=display-resample --interpolation=yes --tscale=oversample video.mp4

5. Verify the Synchronization

While a video is playing, press Shift + I (capital I) to open mpv's on-screen statistics display. Press 2 to switch to the timing graph page.

  • Look for the Mistimed and Delayed counters; they should remain near zero.
  • Check the A/V Sync value; it should remain stable.
  • Check Display Sync status; it should indicate active display timing synchronization.
